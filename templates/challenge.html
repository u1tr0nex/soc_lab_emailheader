🔍 Email Header Analysis Lab

Analyze the raw email headers below and answer the three questions.

{{ headers }}

SPF (Sender Policy Framework): Look for spf= in the Authentication-Results line. It will say pass or fail.

Return-Path domain: Find the Return-Path header. Extract only the domain part after the @ symbol.

Typosquatting tip: Look closely at domains — attackers replace 'm' with 'rn' (e.g. rnicrosoft.com vs microsoft.com). They look identical at a glance!

DKIM: Look for dkim= in the Authentication-Results line. It will say pass or fail.
